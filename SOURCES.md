## Drawings, crosses and logos

The line drawings in `public/images/illustrations/`, the logo marks and the cross are original work made for this template. All crosses come from one SVG source, `src/lib/cross.ts`. The eight-pointed cross has its slanted footrest higher at the viewer's left. They may be used and replaced freely.
